\# UAE AML \& SANCTIONS REGULATORY FRAMEWORK (DIFC \& CBUAE COMPLIANCE)



\## 1. AML LAW NO. 20 (2018) \& CBUAE MANDATES

\- High-Risk Jurisdictions: Transactions involving entities incorporated in FATF high-risk jurisdictions on the enhanced due diligence (EDD) list require mandatory source-of-wealth auditing.

\- Cash/Wire Thresholds: Any single transaction or aggregate linked transactions exceeding AED 55,000 (\~USD 15,000) without verified commercial underlying documentation must be flagged.

\- Shell Company Prohibition: Dealing with corporate vehicles having no physical presence, no operations, and bearer shares is strictly prohibited under Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019.



\## 2. DIFC ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING AND COUNTER-TERRORIST FINANCING (AML) MODULE

\- Section 4.2 (PEP Screening): Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), their immediate family members, and known close associates must be subject to Senior Management approval prior to transaction clearance.

\- Section 5.3 (Sanctions Screening): Authorized firms must cross-check transaction parties against the UAE Consolidated Sanctions List and UN Security Council resolutions without delay.

\- Section 6.1 (Beneficial Ownership): Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) must be determined down to any individual owning or controlling 25% or more of the corporate share capital.



\## 3. ADGM AML AND SANCTIONS RULES

\- Rule 3.4: Mandatory suspicious activity reporting (SAR) to the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) within 48 hours of detecting suspicious behavioral patterns or obfuscated ownership structures.

\- Rule 7.1: Virtual asset transactions exceeding USD 1,000 equivalent require full originator and beneficiary identity verification ("Travel Rule").
